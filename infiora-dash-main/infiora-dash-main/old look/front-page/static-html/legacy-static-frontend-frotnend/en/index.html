Our Process

Go live in days,
not months

Getting Infiora running in your property is refreshingly simple. No complex integrations and no developer required.

01

We Build Your Digital Room Pages

Tell us about your hotel, services, amenities, room types, and brand style. Our team builds the full Infiora setup, including your content, visual identity, and service categories. Most properties go live within 3-5 business days.

✓ Done-for-you setup included
02

Place QR Codes in Every Room

We send print-ready QR codes for every room. Place them on the bedside table, TV stand, or welcome card. Guests scan once and instantly open their full digital concierge in their own language, on any device.

From your dashboard, manage incoming orders in real time, update content across all rooms instantly, and explore deep guest engagement analytics.

As guests use Infiora, the feedback system quietly gathers satisfaction scores, routes happy guests to review platforms, and flags issues before they escalate. Over time, your reputation compounds.
