Platform features

Everything your guests need,
right when they need it.

One guide. Infinite answers. Infiora gives every guest instant access to everything about your property — in their language, on any device.

🍽️

In-room ordering with live tracking

A complete F&B engine inside your guide — categorized menu, items with photos and badges, cart, checkout (cash, card, online), and live order status (Pending → Processing → On the way → Completed) pushed to staff in real time. Works for room service, breakfast in bed, and full restaurant table service.

🍽️
⭐

Smart feedback funnel

A 5-star prompt that routes happy guests to your Google review page and unhappy ones (≤3 stars) to a private detailed feedback form — protecting your reputation while still hearing the truth.

🧹

Housekeeping requests

Guests tap to request cleaning, towels, pillows, amenities, do-not-disturb, extra bed, or a custom item. Staff sees a live queue with status workflow.

🛠️

Maintenance with photo upload

Guests report AC, plumbing, electrical, TV, Wi-Fi, furniture or custom issues — with a photo. Tickets land in the dashboard with full context.

📋

Custom surveys (8 types)

Build any post-stay survey: ratings, yes/no, single & multi choice, NPS, matrix, open text, contact capture. Display as a button or auto-trigger after feedback.

🗺️

Interactive property map

Curate a multilingual map with 15+ marker types — restaurants, beaches, parking, viewpoints, tours — that guests filter and tap for instant directions.

🌐

32 languages, automatic

Every page, every dialog, every menu item — served in the guest's phone language from the very first scan. Croatian, English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Dutch, Czech, Polish, Hungarian, Slovenian, Japanese, Mandarin, Korean, Arabic and more. Manual switcher for the rare exception.

📶

Wi-Fi · Blog · Newsletter

One-tap Wi-Fi credentials, story-style blog sections (galleries, video, embedded maps), and a GDPR-friendly newsletter signup — all built in.

🍴

Restaurant venue mode

Switch from hotel reservation codes to restaurant table PINs — same menu engine, same dashboard, same analytics. One platform, two business models.

📺

Kiosk mode for tablets

A locked-down fullscreen mode that turns any tablet into an in-room concierge surface — perfect for premium suites and restaurant tables.
