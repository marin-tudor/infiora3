1. Who we are

The controller of personal data collected through Infiora.hr and related online activities is Obrt za ugostiteljstvo i usluge Tudors. In this policy, “Infiora”, “Tudors”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the same legal entity and product.

2. What data we collect

We collect data you provide directly and some technical data created when you use the website.

  • Information you enter in the contact form or send by email, such as your name, email address, property name, property type, room count, and message content.
  • Information you share during communication, demo scheduling, inquiries, or support requests.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, browser language, date and time of access, and high-level website usage data.

3. Why we use personal data

We use personal data to respond to inquiries, organize demos, continue business communication, improve the website, and maintain system security.

  • To answer contact requests and continue communication.
  • To prepare demos, offers, or potential cooperation.
  • To analyze and improve website and product performance through Google Analytics.
  • To protect against misuse, spam, and security incidents.

4. Legal basis

We process data when it is necessary to answer your inquiry, take steps before a business relationship, pursue legitimate interests related to security and improvement, and, where required, based on your consent.

5. Automatically collected data and cookies

When you visit Infiora.hr, some information may be collected automatically through browser logs, cookies, and similar technologies. This may include IP address, device type, operating system, browser language, referrer pages, and basic usage information.

More detail is available in our separate Cookie Policy.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your data or disclose it to third parties for marketing purposes. To the extent necessary for the website to function and for traffic measurement, certain technical and analytics data may be processed through Google Analytics and through the technical infrastructure required for website operation and email communication.

7. Retention

We keep data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for follow-up communication, for protection of legitimate business interests, and as required by applicable law.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability of your personal data where the legal conditions for those rights are met.

  • Right of access to the data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to deletion and restriction in certain situations.
  • Right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

9. Contact

If you have any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@infiora.hr. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
