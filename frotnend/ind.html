Our Process

Live in days,
not months

Getting Infiora up and running in your property is refreshingly simple. No complex integrations, no developer required.

01

We Build Your Digital Room Pages

Tell us about your hotel — your services, amenities, room types, and brand style. Our team builds your Infiora setup, including custom content, your visual identity, and all service categories. Most properties go live within 3–5 business days.

✓ Done for you setup included
02

Place QR Codes in Every Room

We send you print-ready QR codes for each room. Display them on the bedside table, TV stand, or welcome card. Guests scan once and instantly access their full digital concierge — in their own language, on any device.

✓ No app download needed
03

Manage Orders & Monitor Analytics

From your dashboard, manage incoming orders in real time, update content across all rooms instantly, and explore deep analytics on guest engagement — what they tap, when they engage, and what makes them satisfied.

✓ Real-time order & insights dashboard
04

Grow Reviews & Revenue

As guests engage with Infiora, our feedback system works quietly in the background — capturing satisfaction scores, routing happy guests to review platforms, and flagging issues before they escalate. Over time, your reputation compounds.

✓ Reputation management on autopilot

Most properties are live within 3–5 business days. Our onboarding team handles the setup — you provide your hotel details, menu items, and brand assets, and we build the pages for you. We can also integrate an optional self-service editor for ongoing updates.

Infiora supports 32 languages out of the box, and automatically detects and serves the guest's browser language. We cover all major European languages (Croatian, English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Dutch, Polish, and more) as well as several non-European options.

Infiora works as a standalone system and does not require PMS integration to function. For enterprise properties that want deeper integration (e.g., auto-populating guest names, linking to reservation systems), we offer custom API connections. Contact us to discuss your setup.

Orders appear instantly on your dashboard and can trigger notifications to your staff (email, browser notification, or integrated display). Infiora is priced as a monthly subscription based on property size. Plans start from €89/month for small properties (up to 15 rooms). Mid-size and enterprise properties receive custom quotes.
