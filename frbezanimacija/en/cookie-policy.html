Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Infiora.hr uses cookies and similar technologies for basic site functionality, security, analytics, and improvement of the user experience.

Last updated: April 5, 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can remember basic settings, support website functionality, and provide insight into how the website is used.

What types of cookies we may use

Necessary cookies

These support core website functionality, security, and stable performance.

Analytics cookies

We use analytics cookies exclusively for Google Analytics so we can understand website usage and improve content, navigation, and performance.

Functional cookies

These may remember basic user settings and technical preferences needed for a smoother experience.

Why we use them

  • To keep the website functional and secure.
  • To analyze usage and improve the user experience.
  • To remember selected settings and technical preferences.
  • To support form-related and communication-related functionality.

How to manage cookies

You can manage most cookies through your browser settings. You may block or delete them, but some parts of the website may not work as intended if you do.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics exclusively for analytics. This helps us understand traffic and user behavior on an aggregated level so we can improve the website and its content.

Contact

If you have any questions about cookies and related data processing, contact us at info@infiora.hr.
